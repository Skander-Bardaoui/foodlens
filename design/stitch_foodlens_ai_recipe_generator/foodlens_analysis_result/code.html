Recipe Steps

1

Prepare & Ferment Dough

Mix flour, water, yeast, and salt. Knead vigorously for 10 minutes until supple, elastic, and smooth. Form into a round tight ball, cover, and let proof for 2 hours at room temperature until doubled in volume.

2

Shape & Stretch

Gently stretch dough by hand from center outward onto semolina flour, preserving the puffy outer ring (cornicione). Avoid using a rolling pin so air bubbles stay trapped inside.

3

Sauce & Cheese

Spoon crushed San Marzano tomatoes evenly outward in gentle spirals. Scatter torn buffalo mozzarella across the base and finish with a delicate drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

4

Bake at Max Heat

Bake on a heavily preheated pizza stone or steel at 500°F (260°C) for 7–9 minutes until the crust is delightfully charred, blistered, and the cheese is fully melted and bubbling.

5

Finish with Fresh Basil

Add fresh sweet basil leaves immediately upon taking the pizza out of the oven so essential oils bloom without blackening. Slice into quarters and serve warmly.

Culinary Master Tip

Draining your fresh mozzarella in a sieve for at least 30 minutes before baking prevents excess moisture from making the center crust soggy.
